## Description

This dataset contains the confirmatory experimental output of the Aegora
Isle simulation platform — a multi-agent reinforcement learning testbed
comparing three governance regimes (No-Governance, Rule-Based, and
Learning-Based/adaptive) for managing a shared, depletable resource across
four environmental scenarios varying in resource abundance and event
frequency.

The dataset consists of **360 replications** (3 governance conditions ×
4 scenarios × 30 replications), each a full training run of a 50-agent
economy over 10,000 timesteps, generated by the project's
`run_experiment.py` confirmatory batch runner.

**Convergence status of each replication is preserved in the metadata**,
not filtered out:

- **295 replications converged** under the study's convergence criterion
  (rolling policy-loss variance below 2.5e-4 over a 20-epoch window, after
  a minimum of 50 epochs). The threshold was recalibrated from the
  originally pre-registered 0.01 — see the methodology note on threshold
  calibration in the source repository.
- **65 replications are recorded as `CONVERGING`**: they ended before
  reaching the 50-epoch minimum needed to evaluate the criterion at all,
  overwhelmingly because the resource pool collapsed early. This is a
  legitimate outcome under the study's collapse-handling protocol, not a
  training failure, and these replications are retained rather than
  excluded — dropping them would remove precisely the events the survival
  analysis exists to measure.

Every replication's status is preserved in `attempts_summary.csv`, together
with its seed and the number of attempts it required. Four replications
needed one replacement attempt at `seed + 1000` under the exclusion
protocol; both the exclusion and the replacement are recorded.

## Collapse: two definitions, and they disagree

The data supports two different collapse definitions, and users should know
which one they are reading:

- The **`collapse_event` column** in each CSV encodes a *subsistence*
  definition (agents falling below a subsistence share of initial mean
  wealth). It fires in 258 of 360 replications.
- The **specification's Table 4 MTSC definition** (resource pool below
  `critical_min`, **or** GDP at zero for three consecutive steps, censored
  at 10,000 steps) yields 81 events: 70 of 120 under No-Governance, 5 of
  120 under Rule-Based, 6 of 120 under Learning-Based.

These point in opposite directions, and the divergence is substantive
rather than an error: governance protects the resource pool while
concentrating wealth away from the poorest agents. The accompanying
statistical analysis uses the specification's Table 4 definition. The
subsistence column is retained in the data so the contrast remains
inspectable.

## File structure

- `README.md` — data dictionary and the limitation above, in full.
- `runs/{G1,G2,G3}/{run_id}.csv` — per-replication timestep-level KPI data
  (Gini coefficient, HDI proxy, GDP-equivalent, depletion rate, resource
  stock, collapse events).
- `runs/{G1,G2,G3}/{run_id}.json` — per-replication metadata (seed,
  governance parameters, scenario parameters, agent-type distribution,
  final KPI means).
- `attempts_summary.csv` — one row per replication: seed, attempts used,
  and final convergence status.
- `cell_attempt_summary.csv` — one row per (governance, scenario)
  combination: replication counts and replacement-attempt statistics.
- `convergence_failures.csv` — audit log of every non-converging attempt
  across the batch, including replaced ones.
- `value_loss_diagnostics.csv` — per-run value-function loss variance,
  recorded observationally as a secondary stability diagnostic. It never
  gated any convergence decision.
- `dataset_manifest.csv` — one row per replication: SHA-256 of both files,
  row count, seed, governance, scenario, and collapse timestep. This makes
  the record self-verifying: a download can be checked for completeness and
  integrity without unpacking the full 423 MB.
